- Summary
  - Big update today! I’ll start with the most exciting change: the MCParks audio client is no longer needed! We now have a built-in audio client with an auto-connect feature and even an audio reconnect feature to help fix when MCParks fails to start the next track! It also has smoother transitions and faster playback compared to the website! A big update has been made to MCParks Better Bins, adding too many textures to mention. This was made possible thanks to Fireball71’s work on the new textures! MCParks Sync has been cleaned up! Including the indicator dot now displayed on the player's head in the player list. A new AFK feature has also been added. When you are inactive for 5 minutes, your dot will change colour so other users know you’re away! Finally, we have two big fixes for our Apple Silicon users! The bug which was causing random text characters not to render upon joining has been fixed, and the One Man’s Dream video mod now actually works and won’t crash you!
    
- Fine-Tuning
  - Sent GPUBooster to the Vault (Was causing missing characters on computers using Apple Silicon.)
  - Reimagined Entity Shadows (They are now disabled by default.)
  - Reimagined 3D Skin Layers (By default, their size is now 1.13; this is to stop them from poking through certain cosmetics.)
  - Updated MCParks Better Bins (Added many new garbage bins! Huge thank you to Fireball71 for their help making the new textures!)
  - Updated OMD Video (Fixed a bug that was causing computers with Apple Silicon to crash or not play the video.)
  - Updated MCParks-AudioConnect (Now known as MCParks Auto Audio. The MCParks audio client is no longer needed. All audio is now handled by the mod!)
  - Updated MCParks Sync (Mostly changes for compatibility with the new backend. Also changed the dots in tab to help with clutter and add an AFK indication function.)
